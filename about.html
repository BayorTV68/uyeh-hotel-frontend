Our story

Built small, on purpose.

Twelve rooms means we know most guests by name before checkout.

Since 2019

A house first, a hotel second.

Solace House started as a private residence on Kuramo Close. When we opened it to guests, we kept the parts that made it feel like a house — a shared kitchen table, a lounge nobody rushes you out of, staff who've mostly been here since the beginning.

We don't run a loyalty program or push upsells at check-in. We just try to get the basics — a good bed, a quiet room, breakfast worth getting up for — right every time.
